What do you mean by change? Is change a matter of time: "I am this, I will be that"? "That" requires time because it is a distance I have to cover. The distance is a measurement, and that measurement is time, and time is created by thought. "I am this, I will be that" – is that change at all? I haven't time: the storm is coming. I don't have time to wait.
