Can I look at myself without distortion? Distortion takes place when I want to achieve something or when I have a motive. When I say, ‘I must understand myself in order to be free,’ I am lost. So can I look at myself without any motive, without any desire to go beyond that which I am? Not to reach enlightenment or understanding – nothing. Those are all carrots hung before a donkey.
